The Service

reCalc is a free tool that helps groups split restaurant and shop bills. It calculates each person's share from the amounts you enter or scan, including a proportional share of tax, service charge and discount.

Check the Results

  • Results are only as accurate as the numbers entered. Always compare the totals with your printed receipt before anyone pays.
  • Receipt scanning uses automatic text recognition, which can misread a value. Every scanned value is shown for you to check and correct before you continue.
  • reCalc is a calculator, not a payment service. It never handles money. The Settle up feature is only a record of who has paid whom; actually paying each other is entirely up to the people involved.

Accounts

  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time from Manage → Account.

Shared Bills

  • Anyone who has the link or QR code of a shared bill can open it, see its items and the names on it, and add their own choices, for 24 hours. Only share it with the people at your table.
  • Do not put private information such as phone numbers or addresses in names or item labels.
