About us / من نحن؟

reCalc is a simple, lightweight web app created to help people easily split receipts and bills between friends, family, or team members. Whether you're out at a café, traveling, or sharing expenses at an event, reCalc makes the process fast, fair, and frustration-free.

This tool was built with simplicity in mind, with no user registration required to use it. Your receipt values are sent securely to our server only to calculate and verify your split — nothing is stored unless you choose to sign in, and even then only your own calculation history is saved to your account.

If you have feedback or ideas to improve reCalc, feel free to reach out through the contact page.
